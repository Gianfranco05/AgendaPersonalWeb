## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Push when a recurring movement is registered or needs confirmation
For templates with `notify = true`, after the scheduler commits newly generated occurrences, the system SHALL send a remote push notification through the existing push infrastructure (Android channel `recordatorios`). One generated transaction SHALL produce the title "Se registró: {name}" with a body including "Ingreso" or "Gasto" and the amount formatted in es-AR with its currency, and data `{type: "recurring_generated", recurrence_id, transaction_id}`. One pending occurrence SHALL produce the title "Confirmá el monto: {name}" with the suggested amount in the body (or an invitation to enter it when there is none), and data `{type: "recurring_confirm", occurrence_id, recurrence_id}`. Occurrences generated by API requests (create, rule change, resume, confirm) SHALL NOT send a push.

#### Scenario: Salary registered
- **WHEN** the scheduler generates the "Sueldo" income of 850.000 ARS for a template with `notify = true`
- **THEN** the user's devices SHALL receive "Se registró: Sueldo" with "$ 850.000,00" in the body

#### Scenario: Electricity to confirm
- **WHEN** the scheduler creates a pending occurrence for "Luz" with suggested amount 25.000
- **THEN** the user's devices SHALL receive "Confirmá el monto: Luz" with data type `recurring_confirm` and the occurrence id

#### Scenario: Notifications disabled
- **WHEN** a template has `notify = false`
- **THEN** its occurrences SHALL be generated without sending a push

#### Scenario: Generated from the app
- **WHEN** a user creates a template whose first occurrence is today
- **THEN** the transaction SHALL be created and no push SHALL be sent

### Requirement: Grouped and non-duplicated recurring notifications
The system SHALL send at most one recurring push per user per scheduler cycle: when a cycle generates more than one notifiable occurrence for the same user, it SHALL send a single summary push ("Movimientos recurrentes", body with the number of registered movements and of movements to confirm, data `{type: "recurring_summary"}`). Only the worker that committed the occurrences SHALL send their push, so concurrent workers SHALL NOT duplicate it. Push delivery failures SHALL be logged and SHALL NOT roll back or block generation.

#### Scenario: Catch-up of several movements
- **WHEN** a cycle generates three transactions and one pending occurrence for the same user
- **THEN** the user SHALL receive exactly one push summarizing 3 registered and 1 to confirm

#### Scenario: Two workers
- **WHEN** two workers process the same due template concurrently
- **THEN** the user SHALL receive exactly one push for that occurrence

#### Scenario: Expo unavailable
- **WHEN** the push provider fails
- **THEN** the generated transactions and pending occurrences SHALL remain persisted and the scheduler SHALL continue with other users

#### Scenario: User without push tokens
- **WHEN** the owner has no registered push token
- **THEN** generation SHALL proceed without error

### Requirement: Opening the app from a recurring notification
The mobile app SHALL route a tapped notification with type `recurring_generated` or `recurring_summary` to Finance › Recurrentes, and with type `recurring_confirm` to Finance › Recurrentes opening the confirmation sheet of that occurrence, both from a running app and from a cold start.

#### Scenario: Tap to confirm
- **WHEN** the user taps "Confirmá el monto: Luz"
- **THEN** the app SHALL open Recurrentes with the confirmation sheet of that pending occurrence prefilled with the suggested amount

#### Scenario: Occurrence already resolved
- **WHEN** the tapped occurrence was already confirmed or skipped from another device
- **THEN** the app SHALL open Recurrentes without the sheet and without error
